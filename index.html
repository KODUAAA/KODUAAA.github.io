01 · About

A builder who likes to understand how things work.

I'm Bright, a BSc Computer Science graduate of the University of Cape Coast, and my graduation ceremony is in November 2026. I'm drawn to the parts of technology that keep systems running and people safe: networks, security and the infrastructure underneath everyday services.

I learn best by building. I built and deployed FEXIWALT, a live ecommerce and classifieds marketplace for Ghana, and Agoloso Motors, a full-stack car dealership platform with its own admin dashboard. On the security side, I've written a network port scanner and a file integrity monitor from scratch in Python, competed in the NestCon capture-the-flag (solving SSRF and SSTI web challenges), and I'm building a machine-learning phishing URL detector for my dissertation. I also like hands-on hardware work: I traced a dead laptop display to a faulty cable and fixed it.

Outside tech, I've done front-line customer service, worked as a mobile money vendor and taught in a classroom. Those jobs taught me reliability, patience with people and how to explain things clearly. I'm now looking for an NSS placement, internship or remote role where I can contribute from day one and keep growing as a technology professional.
